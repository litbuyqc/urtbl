From inspiration to a useful shortlist

How to organize Oopbuy finds.

Use the same four checks for every product. Consistent notes make a shortlist easier to review later.

STEP 01

Choose a category.

Separate sneakers, clothing, headwear and accessories. Save the product link and exact variant rather than relying on a screenshot alone.

STEP 02

Record the facts.

Note the listed size, measurements, color and material description. Mark missing details as unknown instead of guessing.

STEP 03

Inspect the evidence.

Compare available close-ups and quality control photos. Check visible stitching, shape and labels without treating photographs as authentication.

STEP 04

Review the full cost.

Include shipping, service fees and applicable taxes or duties. Confirm product availability and the final service terms before paying.

Quick answers

Before you
follow a find.

Clear answers to common questions about spreadsheet-style product discovery and the scope of this page.

What is an Oopbuy spreadsheet?

An Oopbuy spreadsheet is a product discovery list used to organize shopping links, categories and comparison notes. The label alone does not verify a seller, product quality, authenticity or compatibility with a shopping service.

Does this page provide a downloadable spreadsheet?

No. This page is a category directory and shopping guide. Its shopping links lead to CAFANS, including the specific category pages shown near the top.

How should I compare clothing and shoe sizes?

Compare the listing’s measurements with an item that fits you. For clothing, check chest width, length, waist and inseam as relevant. For shoes, compare foot length with the seller’s size chart and confirm the sizing system.

Do product photos prove authenticity?

No. Photos can help you inspect visible details, but they do not independently prove authenticity, material composition or seller reliability. Check provenance and supporting documentation before buying.

How can I estimate the total shopping cost?

Add item prices, shipping, service fees and applicable taxes or duties using one currency. Confirm the final charges with the relevant seller and service before payment.

Is this an official Oopbuy or designer brand website?

No. This is an independent guide directing visitors to CAFANS. It is not an official Oopbuy, Nike, Louis Vuitton, Gucci or Hermès website. Editorial brand imagery does not establish stock availability, affiliation or endorsement.
